Common Use Cases

📱 Social Media Content
Square (1:1) for Instagram posts, profile pictures
Portrait (3:4) for Instagram Stories, TikTok
Landscape (4:3) for Facebook posts
🎬 Video Content
Video (16:9) for YouTube, Vimeo, standard video embeds
Ultrawide (21:9) for cinematic video content
Portrait (3:4) for mobile-first video content
🌟 Hero Sections
Hero (2:1) for main landing page heroes
Video (16:9) for video backgrounds
Ultrawide (21:9) for dramatic, cinematic headers
🖼️ Product & Gallery Images
Square (1:1) for product grids, thumbnails
Landscape (4:3) for featured products, blog images
Portrait (3:4) for product detail views
👥 Team & Profile Cards
Square (1:1) for team member avatars
Portrait (3:4) for team member profile cards
Landscape (4:3) for team section backgrounds
📰 Blog & Articles
Video (16:9) for featured article images
Hero (2:1) for article headers
Landscape (4:3) for in-content images
