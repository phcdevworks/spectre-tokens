Accent Rails

Every rail has the same eight tones and one shared thickness (0.25rem), so switching the component never means switching the tone name. Where the rail sits is up to the component.

Stepper Tones

Quote

--sp-quote-* covers the text, attribution, an accent-coloured mark, and a rail.

Footer

--sp-footer-* is a dark footer. --sp-footer-light-* has the same roles for a light one.
