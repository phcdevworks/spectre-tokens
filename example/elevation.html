🪜 Elevation & Controls

Elevation levels, control sizing, and compact density

Elevation Levels

Each level pairs a shadow with a surface and a z-index, so you pick one level instead of combining three tokens. The surface follows the active colour mode.

flat --sp-elevation-flat-{shadow,surface,z-index}
raised --sp-elevation-raised-{shadow,surface,z-index}
overlay --sp-elevation-overlay-{shadow,surface,z-index}
modal --sp-elevation-modal-{shadow,surface,z-index}

Control Sizes

Buttons, inputs, and selects share height, inline padding, and icon size per size. Only lg (48px) reaches the 44px minimum touch target, so give smaller controls a larger hit area on touch layouts.
