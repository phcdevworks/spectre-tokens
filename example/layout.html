Article Flow

Spacing between blocks in long-form content, from --sp-layout-prose-flow-*.

A paragraph is followed by --sp-layout-prose-flow-paragraph of space.

Each paragraph after the first gets the same gap.

A heading gets more space above

…and less space below, from --sp-layout-prose-flow-heading-after.

  • List items use --sp-layout-prose-flow-list-item
  • so they stay close together
A quote gets --sp-layout-prose-flow-blockquote above it.

A rule uses --sp-layout-prose-flow-hr above and below.
